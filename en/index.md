## Career

- 1984 Born
- 2003 Entered the University of Tokyo
- 2010 Department of Computer Science, Graduate School of Information Science and Technology, the University of Tokyo (withdrew in 2015)
  - Research area: theory of optimizing compilers for parallel computing
- April 2015 Founded Idein Inc.
- March 2016 Technical Advisor, DG Lab
- September 2018 Selected as an Arm Innovator
- July 2024 Visiting Professor, Center for Co-Creation Strategy, Tohoku University
